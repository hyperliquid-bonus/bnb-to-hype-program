every transfer.

Do I need to connect my wallet?

No wallet connection is required for the described flow. You initiate the transfer from your own wallet.

When should I receive HYPE?

The stated expected delivery time is 4–6 minutes after successful participation. Network conditions and processing can affect timing.

Are calculator results guaranteed?

No. The calculator is an estimate using the displayed base rate and bonus tiers. Confirm the actual contract configuration.

What should I verify?

Verify the destination address, network, payment asset, and program terms. Never share your seed phrase or private keys.
